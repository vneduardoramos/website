## What makes agents different

AI agents aren't just chatbots with better memory. They can sense a problem, reason through data, plan a path forward, coordinate with systems or people, act on decisions, and feed results back into the next iteration.

This makes them more like digital coworkers than tools. They can handle multi-step workflows, freeing human teams to focus on strategy and creativity.

## Why data agents are key

The most impactful category is data agents. Imagine combining a customer table, a contract PDF, and an image into a single analysis. That's what data agents can do: blend structured and unstructured data into reliable insights.

Snowflake highlights three qualities that matter most here: accuracy, efficiency, and governance. Without those, AI breaks trust. With them, AI starts driving ROI.
