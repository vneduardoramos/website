## Content

Datalab is a specialist data consultancy with deep roots in South Africa and a growing global footprint. We believe there is no AI strategy without a data strategy, and we exist to make both achievable for every organisation we partner with.

Founded in South Africa, Datalab has grown into a team of 25+ specialist consultants serving clients across 3 countries. We have built our reputation on one simple principle: demonstrate value before asking for commitment.

50+ Clients Served
15 Years in Business
25+ Specialist Consultants
3 Countries

We started Datalab because we saw too many organisations drowning in data but starving for insight. Our mission is simple: make data useful, accessible, and valuable for every organisation we work with, regardless of their size, sector, or starting point.

Our solutions are built for the people who use them: clear interfaces, guided workflows, and outcomes that speak for themselves. If data is hard to access, it will not be used.

As a Snowflake Elite Partner, we uphold the highest standards of governance, security, and engineering discipline. Our clients rely on us to get it right, every single time.

We design for the modern data ecosystem, integrating sources, teams, and platforms into a single connected layer that drives decisions at every level of your organisation.
