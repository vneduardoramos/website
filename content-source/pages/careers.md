Current opportunities.

Four roles, one team. Find the position that matches where you are in your career, and where you want to go.

Full-time Hybrid

Analytics Engineer

Bridge the gap between raw data and business insight. You will design and maintain analytics models, partner closely with stakeholders to translate business requirements, and ensure our data layer is clean, tested, scalable, and trustworthy.

Strong SQL skills, a modelling mindset, and a passion for self-service analytics are essential. Experience designing dimensional models and enabling modern BI and semantic layers is highly valued.

Sigma ThoughtSpot Power BI Snowflake SQL Data Modelling Kimball Design

Full-time Hybrid

Data Engineer

Build and scale modern cloud data platforms. You will design and develop robust ELT pipelines, integrate complex data sources, and help architect scalable solutions on Snowflake and the modern data stack.

We are looking for engineers with strong SQL and Python skills, a solid understanding of cloud infrastructure, and a passion for building clean, reliable, production-grade data systems.

Python dbt Matillion Openflow Snowflake ELT / ETL Azure AWS

Full-time Hybrid

AI Engineer

Bring AI from experiment to production. You will design and deploy machine learning and generative AI solutions: from feature engineering and model development to serving infrastructure and monitoring. You will work closely with data engineers and analytics engineers to ensure AI is built on a solid data foundation.

We are looking for engineers with strong Python skills, hands-on experience with LLMs, RAG architectures, and ML Ops, and a practical mindset for taking models into production. We believe there is no AI strategy without a data strategy, and you are the link between both.

Python LLMs / RAG ML Ops Machine Learning Vector Databases

Full-time Hybrid

Scrum Master

Be the engine behind high-performing data teams. You will facilitate ceremonies, remove blockers, coach on Agile practices, and create the conditions for engineers and analysts to do their best work. You will act as the connective tissue between technical delivery and stakeholder expectations, keeping momentum high and scope clear.

We are looking for a Scrum Master who thrives in technical environments and genuinely enjoys enabling great teams. Experience working within data or technology delivery teams is a strong advantage. A CSM or equivalent certification is valued, but your track record matters more.

Scrum / Agile Facilitation Jira Stakeholder Management Team Coaching
