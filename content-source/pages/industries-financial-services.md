Financial Services

Your data is more valuable than your products. Treat it that way.

Financial services firms sit on some of the richest data in any sector. Most of it is locked in systems that don't talk to each other, reported on too slowly, and analysed by teams spending most of their time cleaning rather than thinking.

5 clients in this industry

ZA · UK regions delivered

8+ yrs sector experience

The challenges

Where financial services data breaks down

Fragmented data sources: Core banking, policy systems, and CRMs that don't talk to each other create blind spots no dashboard can fix, because the data never arrives clean in the first place.

Our response: Unified cloud data warehouse with governed ingestion from every source system.

Manual compliance reporting: Reports assembled from spreadsheets each quarter carry risk and burn analyst time that should go toward insight, not assembly.

Our response: Automated pipelines and auditable reporting built to your regulatory framework.

Decisions on stale data: Overnight batches and weekly refreshes mean the business always operates on a lag in a market that moves intraday.

Our response: Near-real-time data platforms that cut the lag between event and insight.

What we deliver

Built for the complexity of financial data

Enterprise data warehouses: Cloud-native foundations on Snowflake that scale with your business and don't require rebuilding every two years.

Self-service analytics: Power BI and ThoughtSpot platforms that let your teams answer questions without raising a ticket with data engineering.

Regulatory reporting: Automated, auditable reporting pipelines built to your compliance requirements, from FSCA to FCA.

Analytics and AI use cases: From product evaluations through to production deployment, scoped, de-risked, and delivered with measurable outcomes.
