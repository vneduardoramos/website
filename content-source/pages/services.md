## Content

There is no AI strategy without a data strategy.

Every board is asking about AI. So is every one of your competitors. The organisations moving now are not just ahead on a project. They are building the institutional knowledge, vocabulary, and practices that take years to develop. The ones waiting are not standing still. They are falling behind.

AI strategy is not a one-time decision. It is a long-term organisational capability you either start building now or start losing ground on. The competitive pressure will not ease. What changes over time is how prepared your organisation is when the next shift arrives.

We help CEOs and CTOs move from 'we need an AI strategy' to a grounded, sequenced roadmap. One that accounts for where you actually are, what is genuinely achievable today, and how to build the internal capability that keeps you relevant as the landscape continues to change.

Tier 1, Think: AI & Data Strategy

Start with clarity. Build with conviction.

AI & Data roadmap: Sequenced, prioritised strategy grounded in where you are now. Builds the internal alignment that survives the first board meeting and guides the next three years.

Use case identification: We map AI use cases across your business by department, by data readiness, and by value. You invest in what will actually work, not what sounds impressive in a slide deck.

Agentic AI typology: Not all AI is the same. We help you understand which type applies to which problem, and what data maturity each one requires before it works reliably in production.

The output: A shared conviction about what to do next and why. Not a document that sits in a folder.

AI typology: Not all AI is the same.

Different problems require different types of AI, and each type requires a different level of data maturity before it delivers reliable results in production. Select a type to understand what it needs and where it has worked.

Assistive AI: AI that helps humans decide better

Workflow AI: AI that automates structured processes

Agentic AI: AI that acts autonomously across systems

Generative AI: AI that creates from your data

Every engagement runs as a continuous delivery cycle. Not a project with a handover date. Feedback from the embedded partnership flows directly into delivery priorities. What the business learns shapes what gets built next.

Tier 2, Build: Delivery services

Build the foundation. Deliver from sprint one.

Cloud architecture & data platform: The governed, scalable foundation everything else runs on. We design and build cloud-native data platforms on Snowflake, built for the AI workloads you are planning, not just the reporting you have today. (Snowflake, Matillion, Sigma)

Data engineering & pipelines: Automated, secure data pipelines that connect every source to your warehouse reliably and consistently. The infrastructure that makes real-time AI inference possible. (Snowflake, Matillion, Fivetran)

Analytics & data visualisation: Turning governed data into decisions. We build the reporting and self-service analytics layer that makes insight accessible to everyone who needs to act on it, not just the data team. (Power BI, ThoughtSpot, Sigma)

Embedded analytics: Data as a product. We integrate analytics directly into your applications, client portals, and partner interfaces, turning your data platform into something your customers and partners can see and use. (ThoughtSpot, Power BI)

The output is not a finished product. It is a platform that gets better as the organisation uses it, with every delivery cycle feeding back into what gets prioritised next.

Tier 3, Grow: Embedded partnership

We stay in. And the value compounds.

The embedded partnership is the feedback loop that connects what the business is learning to what gets prioritised in strategy and delivery. We build internal capability and track delivery performance so improvement is continuous, not periodic.

Training & upskilling: Structured programmes that build data and analytics fluency at every level. From executive data literacy to hands-on tool training for analysts and engineers.

Mentorship & peer programming: Senior practitioners embedded in your team, coaching and building capability through doing. Not a training course. A way of working that transfers knowledge with every sprint.

Delivery performance: We track, review, and report on delivery outcomes. Performance data becomes priorities. The feedback loop that keeps what is being built connected to what the business actually needs.

Ongoing skills development: Data capability is not built once. As your platform evolves, as new tools emerge, and as your team grows, we evolve with you. Internal capability stays ahead of the demands the business places on it.

Continuous strategy positioning: The AI and data world is moving fast. We help you stay up to date with industry changes, new technologies, and evolving best practices, so your strategy is always relevant.

A culture of continuous growth. We help you build an organisation that never stands still, always learning, always improving, always optimising performance.

FAQ: Common questions.

Everything you need to know about working with Datalab as your Snowflake and AI partner.

What is a Snowflake Elite Partner? The Snowflake Partner Programme has three tiers, Select, Premier, and Elite. Elite is the highest tier, reserved for partners with the deepest technical expertise, the strongest verified delivery track record, and the most active client relationships. Datalab is the only Elite Partner headquartered in South Africa.

How does Snowflake enable AI strategy? Snowflake Cortex brings large language models and ML functions directly into your data platform, no separate AI infrastructure required. Datalab helps organisations identify the right AI use cases, build the data foundation to support them, and deploy Cortex-powered solutions in production.

How long does a Snowflake implementation take? A typical Snowflake implementation runs 8 to 16 weeks for an initial production platform, depending on data volume, source system complexity, and the number of analytics use cases in scope. We deliver value from sprint one, not at handover.

Can Datalab migrate our existing data warehouse to Snowflake? Yes. Migration is one of our most common engagements. We have migrated on-premise data warehouses, Azure SQL environments, and legacy BI platforms onto Snowflake for organisations including Sanlam and Agrihub. We manage the full technical delivery and programme governance.

What does Snowflake and AI consulting cost in South Africa? Engagements are scoped and priced based on complexity, team size, and duration. We offer fixed-cost, time-and-materials, and team augmentation models. Contact us for a no-obligation scoping conversation.

How is Datalab different from other Snowflake partners in Africa? Datalab is the only Snowflake Elite Partner based in South Africa. Elite status is awarded to fewer than 5% of partners globally and requires a verified track record of successful deliveries. We also operate as an Authorised Reseller, meaning clients can purchase Snowflake licences directly through us.

Is Snowflake right for our organisation's size? Snowflake scales in both directions. We work with large enterprises and with organisations that are moving off spreadsheets and legacy tools for the first time. If you have data you are not using well, or reporting that takes too long, Snowflake is likely a fit. Our scoping conversations are free and honest: if it is not the right tool for where you are today, we will tell you.
